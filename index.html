Privacy Policy

Subliminal Recode · Lucid Apps · Last updated 27 August 2026

The short version: Subliminal Recode does not collect your personal information, does not connect to the internet, and does not send anything anywhere. Your affirmation messages and settings are stored only on your own PC, and nobody else can see them — including us.

This policy explains what Subliminal Recode does with your information. It is short because the app does very little with it.

Information we collect

None. Subliminal Recode collects no personal information of any kind. There are no accounts to create, no sign-in, no profile, and nothing to fill in about yourself in order to use the app.

The app also contains no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party tracking components of any kind.

The app does not use the internet

Subliminal Recode has no network functionality whatsoever. It does not make network connections, does not upload or download anything, and does not check for updates on its own — updates are delivered by the Microsoft Store. Because nothing ever leaves your PC, there is no transmission of your data to protect, intercept or misuse.

What is stored on your own PC

Everything the app saves stays on your computer, in the app's own private data folder. That is:

  • Your messages and categories — the built-in affirmations plus any you add or edit yourself.
  • Your settings — flash duration, frequency, position, opacity, font, size, style and colour, and whether the app runs when Windows starts.
  • Automatic backups — before you delete a category or a message, the app saves a snapshot of your message library so a mistake can be undone. The five most recent are kept and older ones are removed automatically.
  • A recovery copy — if the app's saved data file is ever found to be damaged, a copy of it is kept alongside rather than being overwritten, so nothing is lost silently.

These files are readable by you and by anyone with access to your Windows user account, in the same way as any other document on your PC. They are not encrypted, and they are not designed to hold sensitive information — please bear that in mind if you write personal details into your own affirmations.

Files you export yourself

The app can export your messages to a plain text file so you can back them up, move them to another PC, or share them. That file is saved wherever you choose to put it, and from that point it is an ordinary file under your control. If you share it, you are sharing whatever you wrote in it. The app plays no part in this beyond writing the file you asked for.

Purchases and the Microsoft Store

Subliminal Recode is sold through the Microsoft Store. Your purchase, payment details, free trial and licence are handled entirely by Microsoft, and are covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement rather than by this policy. We never see your payment information.

As the publisher, Microsoft provides us with summary reports about the app — things like how many copies were bought, in which countries, and any ratings and reviews people chose to leave publicly. These reports do not identify individual customers to us. If you contact us for support, we will of course see whatever you choose to tell us in your message.

Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose your information, because we do not have any to share. No data is passed to advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers or any other third party.

Children

Subliminal Recode is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

Your rights over your data

Because the app stores your information only on your own PC and sends none of it to us, your data is already entirely in your hands. You can view, edit, export or delete your messages and settings at any time from within the app. Uninstalling Subliminal Recode through Windows removes the app together with the data it saved.

Depending on where you live you may have legal rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase — but if you have a question about this, you are welcome to get in touch using the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address with an updated date at the top. Any change that affects how the app handles your information will also be described in the app's Store listing update notes.
